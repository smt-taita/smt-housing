# Replit Prompt: St Matt's Taitā Housing Project Fundraising Website

Create a single-page static fundraising website for the St Matt's Taitā Housing Project using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. This is a church-led affordable housing initiative in New Zealand that needs ongoing funding support.

## Project Overview
**Campaign:** St Matt's Taitā Community Housing Project  
**Goal:** Raise $24,000 annually to maintain affordable rent subsidies  
**Urgency:** Early subsidies end in early 2026  
**Target:** 20 donors at $100/month (or equivalent combinations)  
**Audience:** Wider Anglican church networks and community supporters

## Design Requirements

### Visual Style
- **Tone:** Warm, hopeful, professional but approachable
- **Colors:** Use warm, welcoming colors with Anglican church sensibilities
  - Primary: Deep blue or green (trust/stability)
  - Accent: Warm orange or gold (hope/community)
  - Supporting: Soft grays and whites
- **Typography:** Clean, readable fonts that work well in both English and te reo Māori
- **Layout:** Mobile-first responsive design

### Key Sections (in order)

#### 1. Hero Section
- **Headline:** "St Matt's Kāinga - More Than Housing, We're Building Community"
- **Subheading:** "Help us keep 8 warm, dry homes affordable for families in need"
- **Hero video embed:** Responsive video player (YouTube/Vimeo ready) with fallback hero image
- **Video overlay:** Play button and "Watch Our Story" text
- **Primary CTA:** Large "Support Our Community" button
- **Visual progress bar:** "$X raised of $24,000 annual goal"

#### 2. The Story Section
**Title:** "A Village of Welcome"
**Content:** 
- Brief story of transformation from "patch of grass" to thriving community
- Mention: 8 homes (1x 4-bed, 3x 2-bed, 4x 1-bed)
- Residents: families, former refugee, people from transitional housing
- Two church leaders living onsite as community catalysts
- Part of Anglican Diocese Bedrock Housing initiative

#### 3. The Challenge Section
**Title:** "Why We Need Your Help"
**Content:**
- Early subsidies ending in early 2026
- $24,000 needed annually for rent relief
- Focus on one-bedroom units for lowest-income tenants
- Without support, some residents may need to leave
- Urgency: fundraising needed by January 2026

#### 4. How to Help Section
**Title:** "Join Our Village of Support"
**Donation options with clear buttons:**
- $20/month ($240/year)
- $50/month ($600/year) 
- $100/month ($1,200/year) - marked as "Preferred"
- $200/month ($2,400/year)
- Custom amount option
- One-time donation option

**Additional ways to help:**
- Prayer support
- Volunteer skills
- Share with networks
- Church/workplace partnerships

#### 5. Impact & Transparency
**Title:** "Your Impact"
- Tax-deductible status (33.33% IRD tax credit in NZ)
- Quarterly updates promise
- Managed by St Matt's Taitā Church Committee
- Contact: housing@stmattstaita.org.nz

#### 6. FAQ Section (Collapsible)
Include key FAQ points:
- What is the project?
- Who lives there?
- Why ongoing donations needed?
- How much should I give?
- Is it tax-deductible?
- Will I receive updates?

#### 7. Footer
- Contact information
- Anglican Diocese of Wellington connection
- "More than homes—we're building hope"

## Functional Requirements

### Donation Processing
- **Payment integration:** Create form structure ready for Stripe/PayPal integration
- **Recurring donation options:** Monthly giving emphasized
- **One-time donations:** Also available
- **Donation amount buttons:** Pre-set amounts with custom option
- **Form fields:** Name, email, phone, donation amount, frequency, message (optional)

### Progress Tracking & Campaign Management

#### Real-Time Progress Display
- **Dynamic progress bar:** Visual representation of campaign progress
- **Multiple metrics display:**
  - Total raised vs $24,000 annual goal
  - Number of donors/supporters
  - Percentage complete
  - Monthly recurring commitments secured
  - Time remaining until subsidy deadline (January 2026)

#### Data Source Integration
**Automated Online Tracking:**
- **Payment processor integration:** Automatic updates from Stripe/PayPal
- **API endpoints:** Ready for webhook integration from donation platforms
- **Real-time updates:** Progress bar updates immediately after online donations

**Manual Admin Updates:**
- **Admin dashboard:** Simple, secure admin panel for manual updates
- **Offline donation tracking:** Easy form to add:
  - Donor name (optional/anonymous)
  - Amount pledged
  - Frequency (one-time, monthly, annual)
  - Date received
  - Notes field
- **Bulk import:** CSV upload capability for multiple offline pledges
- **Edit/delete:** Ability to modify or remove entries if needed

#### Campaign Analytics Dashboard
**Admin-only section with:**
- **Fundraising velocity:** Daily/weekly progress charts
- **Donor breakdown:** Online vs offline contributions
- **Recurring vs one-time:** Monthly commitment tracking
- **Geographic data:** Where supporters are located (if collected)
- **Average donation:** Statistical insights
- **Export functionality:** Download supporter data for thank-you communications

#### Public Progress Features
- **Live counter:** Updates every few seconds
- **Recent activity:** "Recent supporters" section (privacy-conscious)
- **Milestone celebrations:** Visual celebrations at 25%, 50%, 75% complete
- **Social sharing of progress:** "We're at 60% - help us reach our goal!"
- **Urgency indicators:** "Only $X remaining to secure housing for 2025"

### Social Sharing
- **Share buttons:** Facebook, email, WhatsApp (popular in NZ)
- **Pre-written social text:** "Help St Matt's Taitā keep affordable homes available for families in need"
- **Shareable images:** Space for campaign graphics

### Contact & Engagement
- **Email signup:** Newsletter/updates list
- **Contact form:** Questions and partnership inquiries
- **Email link:** Direct link to housing@stmattstaita.org.nz
